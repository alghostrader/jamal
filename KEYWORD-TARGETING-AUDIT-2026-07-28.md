## What to STOP targeting (near-zero volume, dedicated pages not justified)
- FR: "iptv sans coupure" (90), "iptv firestick" (140) as standalone money angles, hyper-niche legality variants.
- NL: "legale iptv" (110), "iptv proefaccount" (~0) as primary targets — keep as sections, not campaigns.
- ES: the many <200-volume telegram/legal variants beyond the one winner.
These aren't *wrong* to exist (they catch long-tail), but they shouldn't absorb net-new effort while 6k–50k terms sit untargeted.

## Priority actions (highest volume-per-effort first)
1. **Build smarters-live.com around "iptv smarters pro" (49,500/mo)** — install/app-guide hub → funnel to primeiptv. Biggest untapped stream in the portfolio.
2. **iptvned: create/optimise "iptv kopen" (6,600) + "iptv aanbieders" (3,600) pages** — replace effort currently going to 110-volume legal terms.
3. **iptvesp: push "iptv españa" (4,400) from #49** with internal links from the Telegram winner — one page, big upside.
4. **primeiptv (FR flagship): strengthen "boitier iptv" (14,800), "application iptv" (6,600), "abonnement iptv" (27,100)** — it already has the pages; needs links + on-page depth.
5. **Everywhere: stop spawning sub-200-volume posts.** Add a "minimum 300 searches/mo or clear buy-intent" rule to the content workflow, same spirit as the title-length guard.
