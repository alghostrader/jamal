Standing strategy

  • FR flagship = primeiptv. All French links and content investment go here until further notice.
  • iptvesp = protect & monetise. The Telegram cluster carries the traffic; the money page must stay fast and linked.
  • iptvpix = recovering. No penalty; the cause was authority/crawl-demand. Domain Rank is now measurable and Semrush lists it again. No restructuring.
  • Satellites stay in their lanes — spf install/config, ifo premium/HDR, aio trial/deals. One keyword, one page, one site.
  • Authority is the portfolio ceiling. Referring domains per site are still in the single digits on the flagship — the Backlinks steps are the highest-leverage work available.
  • AI search is a live channel. ChatGPT already cites the portfolio; write every article so it can be cited (numbered steps, direct answers, tables).
