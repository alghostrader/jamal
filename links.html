Journalist pitch template (Mon)

JOURNALIST PITCH TEMPLATE — Source of Sources / Featured / Qwoted
(Identity: streaming-technology & cord-cutting cost expert. NEVER "IPTV subscription seller".)

SUBJECT: Re: [their query title]

Hi [first name],

[1 sentence that directly answers their question — the quotable line.]

[2-3 sentences of substance: a number, a country comparison, or a practical tip. Examples:
"IPTV as a technology is legal across the EU — what determines legality is whether the streams
are licensed; the per-country differences are mostly about enforcement." /
"A household in Spain switching from cable to licensed streaming typically cuts the bill 40-60%." /
"The #1 setup mistake we see is choosing the wrong app for the device — compatibility varies by
platform."]

If useful, we maintain a country-by-country IPTV legality reference and a device-compatibility
checker, free to cite: [link to /iptv-legal or /application-iptv].

Credentials: [name], founder of [brand] ([site]), a licensed-content IPTV service operating in
Spain/France/Netherlands. Happy to expand on any point before your deadline.

[name] · [site] · [email]

RULES: only reply when genuinely on-topic (off-topic = banned on SoS) · answer within hours,
not days · 2-3 pitches/week max · plain text, no attachments.

Outreach templates (Fri)

RECLAIM & LISTICLE OUTREACH TEMPLATES (Friday routine, 5 emails max)

A) UNLINKED MENTION →
Subject: Thanks for mentioning [brand]
Hi [name] — saw you mentioned [brand] in [article URL]. Thanks! Would you mind linking the
mention to https://[site]/ so your readers can find it? Happy to return the favour with a quote
or data for a future piece. — [name]

B) LISTICLE ("best IPTV apps / meilleur IPTV / beste IPTV" pages ranking top-20) →
Subject: Addition for your [title] list
Hi [name] — nice roundup. One gap: [specific missing thing]. We built a free
[device-compatibility checker / legality-by-country reference] your readers might use:
[tool URL]. If it earns a spot, great; either way, one factual correction for you: [tiny
genuine correction]. — [name]

C) BROKEN LINK →
Subject: Dead link in [article]
Hi [name] — the link to [dead URL] in [article] is returning 404. We keep a live equivalent
here: [our URL]. Might be a quick fix for your readers. — [name]
